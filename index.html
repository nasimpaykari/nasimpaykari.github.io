Doctoral Research Program

Cooperative Autonomy & Resilient Consensus

My doctoral research addresses the dual vulnerability of autonomous fleets: perceptual ambiguity in GPS-denied physical space, and Byzantine faults in multi-agent communication networks.

Cross-View Landmark Recognition

Developing deep feature factorization and invariant landmark representations that allow multi-robot visual homing to converge reliably even under extreme perspective distortion and lighting shifts.

RoboStake & Witness-BFT

Pioneering proof-of-navigation consensus with cryptographic signature trees, ensuring robotic teams agree on verified state estimates while mathematically proving safety under up to 33% malicious nodes.
