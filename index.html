Nasim Paykari

Cooperative robotics, visual navigation & cyber-physical systems

I study how robotic teams navigate and coordinate in GPS-denied environments. My research brings together shared visual landmarks, decentralized pathfinding, blockchain consensus, and agentic AI for wide-area visual homing. I also bring more than a decade of industrial control experience, connecting software, communication networks, and physical equipment.

Research Experience

Doctoral Researcher

Robotics and Computer Vision Laboratory, Fordham University · During Ph.D. studies, 2022–present

Co-advisers: Damian M. Lyons and Mohamed Rahouti.
  • Currently investigate agentic artificial intelligence for wide-area visual homing.
  • Conduct research on Wide Area Visual Navigation (WAVN), enabling cooperative robot teams to navigate toward out-of-view destinations using shared visual landmarks in GPS-denied environments.
  • Develop and evaluate blockchain-based coordination and consensus methods for cooperative visual homing, including RoboStake and Witness Byzantine Fault Tolerance with Proof-of-Navigation.
  • Investigate decentralized pathfinding and route recovery on shared landmark graphs, including alternative-path strategies for changing connectivity and navigation failures.
  • Study cross-view landmark recognition across widely separated viewpoints to identify common landmarks observed by different robots.
  • Implement and evaluate navigation experiments primarily in ROS and Gazebo, with additional work on Turtle robot platforms.
